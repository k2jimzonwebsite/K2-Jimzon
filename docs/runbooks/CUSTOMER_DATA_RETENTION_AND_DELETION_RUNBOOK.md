### Proposed independent closure journal — MAP-019 / IDEA-20261027-02

**Understanding and assumptions:** This extends the existing owner-approved automatic closure, not a new customer model. Its purpose is to stop an older backup from silently reactivating a closed account or restoring eligible erased content. Customers use the existing closure control; staff recover a system offline and never need customer contact values in the journal. The current database cannot be the only source of closure evidence because it may be lost or restored to an earlier point. The storage provider, custody, throughput, pricing and a trustworthy independently held latest-version witness are not yet selected or observed. Until those are qualified, activation stays blocked. Existing Excel files are outside this integration; they are not erased by this design. No numeric financial retention policy is assumed.

**Options considered:** (1) Export tombstones only after closure: simplest but a crash between deletion and export loses the only evidence and is rejected. (2) A journal in the same database/backup: insufficient after a disaster and rejected. (3) A separately durable, authenticated intent/commit journal with restore held offline until its complete newest state is independently verified: preferred for further review; no provider or deployment is implied.

**Proposed protocol J1–J5; unimplemented:**

- J1. A verified, eligible closure writes a minimal durable intent into the same database transaction as the decision; fields identify the K2 project, original Auth UUID, canonical customer UUID if any, request UUID, applicable erasure-policy version and intended action, never contact values, OTPs, conversation bodies or raw tokens. Intent is not a closure receipt and does not itself erase or revoke access. Cancellation remains possible until irreversible work starts.
- J2. An authenticated server-only exporter commits the exact intent to separately durable append-only storage and receives a verifiable durable acknowledgement before irreversible database closure. A pre-send outbox makes retries idempotent; acknowledgement loss is reconciled by the same event identity, not by inventing an event. A missing or untrusted acknowledgement keeps closure pending rather than falsely claiming completion. This may add latency and must be reconciled with the user's immediate-processing goal by measuring the actual provider path before activation.
- J3. Under the existing actor/customer/request locks, recheck verified identity, cancellation, all current holds and the exact acknowledged intent. Only then perform the already-reviewed minimization/access denial and write a closure outcome to the outbox. A later authenticated journal outcome records the committed policy/erasure categories. If the system fails after a journaled intent but before a trustworthy outcome, the intent remains ambiguous: recovery must fail closed for that identity and request staff reconciliation, never assume either cancellation or successful deletion.
- J4. Before a restored database is reachable by users, obtain the complete journal and a separately trusted, monotonic newest-head witness. Authenticate project, sequence, integrity and continuity, and prove the head covers the intended recovery point. A journal's own signed old head cannot establish that later events do not exist. Missing, stale, incomplete, ambiguous or unverifiable evidence keeps the restored service offline; no operator override is specified here.
- J5. Reapply verified committed closures to the original Auth and canonical customer UUIDs, preserving required transaction evidence and access-denial tombstones; do not use a reused email or phone to identify a later account. Old backups may show stale open orders or pre-closure schema: restore reconciliation must not simply rerun ordinary eligibility on that stale snapshot or reactivate access. Map each committed policy version and affected field/category explicitly; unknown schema, unmapped attachments or incomplete decisions fail closed. Exercise restore and replay repeatedly on isolated synthetic databases before any production use.

**Decision log, pending structured review:** Separate journal plus independent latest-head witness is preferred; rejected alternatives are recorded above. Journal/provider custody, witness independence, commit/outcome crash recovery, field-level restore mapping, operation latency, monitoring and resource limits remain review and activation gates. All work stays in MAP-019; this text is a design proposal, not a completed implementation or proof that any copy has been erased.

**Provider capability research — 27 October; no K2 account or configuration observed:** For the existing MAP-019 decision-authority problem, Cloudflare documents that direct R2 API/binding reads, object listings and writes are strongly consistent, and competing `If-None-Match: *` writes to a previously absent key have at most one successful winner. Its documentation also states a negative read does **not** settle an earlier still-in-flight write; a same-key conditional retry can reconcile an ambiguous create. Cached custom-domain access has different consistency and must not be substituted for direct API access. [Cloudflare R2 consistency](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/reference/consistency/). This makes an R2 create-once decision object a candidate primitive for a specific same-key race, not a complete implementation of request registration, per-account uniqueness, generation fencing, committed-freeze proof, independent witness, authenticated replay or a safe response to a missing object. No claim is made that conditional creation alone resolves those requirements.

Cloudflare documents that R2 bucket locks prevent overwrite/deletion of covered objects, but a sufficiently privileged configuration operator can remove a lock rule. The duration and permission model require owner-reviewed retention and separate custody; neither indefinite retention nor an immutable witness is assumed. [Cloudflare bucket locks](https://developers.cloudflare.com/r2/buckets/bucket-locks/). SQLite-backed Durable Objects document strongly consistent transactional storage and point-in-time recovery, but recovery can restore older state; a separately trusted current head and real failover/writer barriers still need qualification. External I/O inside a Durable Object can interleave requests, so a network round trip does not become part of its atomic storage transaction. [Durable Object storage](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/api/sqlite-storage-api/), [Durable Object concurrency guidance](https://developers.cloudflare.com/durable-objects/best-practices/rules-of-durable-objects/). Workers KV's eventual consistency makes it unsuitable as the sole current-decision authority. [Workers KV consistency](https://developers.cloudflare.com/kv/concepts/how-kv-works/).

## Non-negotiable boundary

Customer deletion must not erase order, payment, quotation, fulfillment,
inventory-reservation, consignment, tax/accounting, security, or dispute truth.
A matching email, phone, name, account, or URL identifier is not sufficient
authority to erase a canonical customer. Requests require verified account
ownership or attributable staff verification, and every decision must be
audited without copying the subject's PII into the audit event.

The current identity migration deliberately uses `ON DELETE RESTRICT` from
customer contact points, account links, channel identities, guest grants, claim
requests, order requests, Pasabuy requests, and conversations to canonical
customers. That protection stays in place. A future implementation must
minimize or anonymize eligible PII while preserving required operational rows;
it must never solve retention by cascading deletion from `customers`.

   Permitted manual exceptions require Admin+AAL2 approval. Both paths require
   an attributable decision/policy basis, a bounded allowlisted plan, concurrency
   control, and immutable before/after counts; preview manual decisions first.
6. Revoke account and guest access, terminate sessions, execute the approved
   plan transactionally, and return a public request reference—not internal IDs
   or deleted values.
7. Verify customer/account/guest denial and retained operational integrity.
   Record backup expiry implications and provide a truthful completion or
   retained-data response.

### Engineering assumptions to validate before implementation

Automatic processing means fixed backend rules; current volumes and worker
limits are unmeasured. Use one scoped request with bounded batches and a durable
policy-versioned decision; no unbounded bulk erasure. Immediate access removal
means authoritative account/guest/session denial once the request is eligible,
not merely clearing browser state. Slow work runs with durable checkpoints and
retry/uncertainty reconciliation; no false completion after timeout. Owner-
approved automatic resumption rechecks current holds/identity linkage without
requiring another expression of closure intent. A cancelled or superseded
request cannot be resumed as if still active. Cancellation before irreversible
removal is now owner-approved; the ownership check and execution/cancellation

relink erased information through reused contact details or prior guest state.

### Official-source research — evidence, not an adopted K2 schedule

- [Data Privacy Act, section 11](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act-/)
  and [IRR, section 19](https://privacy.gov.ph/implementing-rules-regulations-data-privacy-act-2012/):
  retention must follow a necessary declared purpose or other justified basis;
  indefinite storage for an unspecified possible future use is not a schedule.
- [BIR RR 7-2024, section 4](https://bir-cdn.bir.gov.ph/BIR/pdf/RR%20No.%207-%202024.pdf):
  books and supporting accounting records generally have a five-year rule,
  measured from the specified return-filing reference for the last-entry year;
  relevant pending cases can require longer preservation. This is not a
  blanket five-year rule for all K2 profiles, chats or delivery-address copies.
  Cousin/bookkeeping review must map K2 record classes and dates; no production
  deletion clock or business-specific legal conclusion is approved here.
- [NPC Advisory 2021-01, sections 5 and 10](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SGD-Advisory-DS-Rights-29-Jan-2021.pdf):
  requests need reasonable identity checks, timely handling and justified
  retention decisions; erasure/blocking covers live and backup systems. Its
  response provisions use 30 working days and a conditional additional 15,
  with notice. A held closure must not silently stop request tracking or
  communication. Backup expiry alone is not established compliance evidence.

The backup runbook's 14 daily/8 weekly/12 monthly policy remains a conditional
target, not a verified schedule or deletion guarantee. Inventory every retained
copy, prevent restored data from silently reactivating erased profiles, and
verify the approved expiry/redaction process before claiming backup completion.
Meta's current deletion-callback documentation could not be fetched (HTTP 429);

## Draft processing and recovery boundary

Recommended approach remains the existing K2 backend with one durable request.
The owner has confirmed the business workflow; concrete technical design and
retention matrix are not yet accepted or implemented.

Initial fresh proof binds one requester and intended customer scope. After
durable receipt, current eligibility sends routine work to automatic processing
or open-case/uncertain work to a visible hold with an assigned Admin. A hold
does not waive timely responses. Case resolution rechecks the same active
request; no repeated closure consent is required. Cancellation and irreversible
start must serialize: once cancellation wins, queued retries must not execute;
once irreversible processing starts, erased information cannot be restored.

Access denial and eligible database minimization have separate durable outcomes
from Auth, attachment, Excel and backup work. Resolve ambiguous remote results
before retrying. A failed external operation must not reopen already closed
access or repeat irreversible database work. Preserve retained transaction
integrity and the original request identity through retry/recovery.

For Excel, a future approved file connection may automate classified cleanup;
without observed access, route that step to the assigned Admin/custodian and
retain an external-copy-pending status. Do not claim complete erasure merely
because app access is closed or the database step passed. Report completed
scope, retained categories/bases and pending copies truthfully. This manual
external exception does not replace the owner's automatic routine policy.

Fresh re-registration requires new identity proof and no automatic resurrection
of removed profile/message data. Access to legitimately retained old records
requires its own authorized scope; a matching contact value alone grants none.
Operational implementation still requires exact source/schema reconciliation,
