## Findings

### F01 — Public navigation has no crawlable destinations

- **Category:** Crawlability & Indexation. **Severity:** High. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−10.
- **Evidence:** Rendered Pasabuy and Contact have zero same-origin anchors. Home has one distinct product destination and no service anchors. Catalog has116distinct product links, but zero non-product internal anchors. Main/mobile menu and service/policy/footer navigation are buttons in StoreHeader.jsx, Footer.jsx and associated components. The existing product-card repair works.
- **Why it matters:** A sitemap helps discovery but does not replace a useful internal-link hierarchy. Google cannot reliably discover destinations through button click handlers. [Google crawlable-link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).
- **Recommendation:** Use real canonical links for public destinations while retaining existing client navigation and native modified-click/keyboard behavior. Keep cart, theme, filtering and other actions as buttons. Preserve private-route exclusions. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-05, merge into MAP-024.

### F02 — All submitted pages depend on JavaScript for body content

- **Category:** Crawlability & Indexation. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−5.
- **Evidence:** All111raw responses contain zeroH1 elements and zero internal body anchors. Existing build helpers prerender head metadata/JSON-LD, with an empty root. Rendered Pasabuy is available to Google, so a current rendering failure is not inferred.
- **Why it matters:** Rendering and runtime/API dependencies remain necessary to obtain meaningful content and product links. The earlier optional-chunk Soft404 demonstrated this dependency's practical risk; that earlier defect is repaired. [Google JavaScript SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/javascript/javascript-seo-basics).
- **Recommendation:** Assess meaningful initial body/prerendered public content through the existing rendering boundary, preserving freshness and one canonical catalog. A framework rewrite or crawler-only alternate site is not justified. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-07, retain as a later part of existing discovery work after smaller fixes.

### F03 — Contact and policy routes inherit generic homepage metadata

- **Category:** On-Page Optimization. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−5.
- **Evidence:** Initial Contact,Privacy,Terms,Returns andStore responses use the homepage title/description/canonical. Rendered Contact corrects its canonical to/contact but still keeps the homepage title and generic description. The shared SEO table names only Home,Catalog,Pasabuy andTrade.
- **Why it matters:** Different public pages have indistinguishable search/share titles and conflicting initial versus rendered canonical signals. Store may intentionally consolidate; it needs an explicit canonical decision rather than an accidental fallback.
- **Recommendation:** Extend the existing shared public-route metadata contract for actual trust/contact pages; explicitly decide the legacy Store canonical. Reuse build/runtime metadata rather than creating a parallel SEO system. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-07, merge into MAP-024.

### F04 — Mobile main-content loading is slow in both sampled pages

- **Category:** Technical Foundations. **Severity:** High. **Confidence:** High for lab result; field impact unmeasured. **Score impact:**−10.
- **Evidence:** Home LCP7.1s/performance72; Pasabuy LCP8.4s/performance55. Home transfers6,847KiB, with3,748KiB estimated image-delivery savings. One product PNG alone transfers2,119.9KiB. Home's LCP node is the optional Analytics paragraph; Pasabuy's is the hero video/poster. PSI flags priority/discovery for that video resource. No claim that image weight is the sole root cause.
- **Why it matters:** Visitors on constrained mobile connections wait for useful content; good bundle budgets alone do not establish a fast whole page.
- **Recommendation:** Optimize responsive derivatives of approved photos/wood assets while preserving original packaging pixels/colors/alpha; prioritize the actual first-view poster/content and reduce competing delivery. Qualify consent's early rendering without loading Google before acceptance or hiding required choices. Preserve the established identity. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-06, merge into MAP-024.

### F05 — Pasabuy shifts substantially during the lab page load

- **Category:** Technical Foundations. **Severity:** High. **Confidence:** High for lab result; field frequency unmeasured. **Score impact:**−10.
- **Evidence:** Pasabuy CLS0.399; Home0.048. PSI identifies moved footer/header content and Fraunces/Source Sans web-font contributions. This diagnoses a load/stability target, not a complete root-cause proof for every shift.
- **Why it matters:** Moving content makes reading and choosing controls less predictable.
- **Recommendation:** Stabilize first-view layout and font fallback/loading dimensions, then remeasure the same mobile scenario. Keep the existing fonts and accessible consent behavior. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-06.

### F06 — Homepage and Pasabuy make inconsistent freight claims

- **Category:** Content Quality & E-E-A-T. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−5.
- **Evidence:** Live Home says approved Pasabuy custom items are transported by scheduled air cargo, under “Scheduled monthly cargo flights to Manila” (StorySection.jsx:25–28). The approved Pasabuy page offers agreed sea or air freight and item-specific timing.
- **Why it matters:** Inconsistent service promises undermine trust and the customer's understanding of the offer. This is not a claim of a Google penalty.
- **Recommendation:** Align the Home story with the approved quote/method/timing contract; preserve genuinely confirmed regular stock-shipment facts separately and avoid inventing a schedule. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-08, merge into MAP-024.

### F07 — Pasabuy lacks several service-decision answers on its canonical page

- **Category:** Content Quality & E-E-A-T. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−5.
- **Evidence:** Pasabuy's main text has279words including form labels, badges and four process steps. It does not explain quote components, request limitations, delivery/pickup coverage or provide a worked genuine request example. Home has broader FAQs, but no crawlable link to this service page. Word count itself is not an error or ranking target.
- **Why it matters:** A visitor searching for Italy-to-PH Pasabuy needs useful answers before sending contact details. [Google people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).
- **Recommendation:** Add concise, verified service answers and approved first-hand examples on/pasabuy. Use the owner's target phrases naturally. Do not invent fees, schedules, import/legal assurances, reviews, shipment proof orFAQ rich-result promises. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-08; exact facts/copy need review before publication.

### F08 — The public business identity is not represented in structured data

- **Category:** On-Page Optimization. **Severity:** Low. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−3.
- **Evidence:** Live Home/Pasabuy/Contact have no JSON-LD; sampled product pages contain Product andBreadcrumbList only. Public contact/social details exist visibly.
- **Why it matters:** Organization/OnlineStore data can help Google identify the business and relevant logo/contact/social profiles; it is optional and no ranking guarantee. [Google Organization documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/organization).
- **Recommendation:** Describe verified business identity through one shared structured-data source. Keep private warehouse/address details private unless expressly approved for public use. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-09, merge into existing metadata boundary.

### F09 — Product schema has valid basics but omits useful public facts

- **Category:** On-Page Optimization. **Severity:** Medium. **Confidence:** High. **Score impact:**−5.
- **Evidence:** All116sampled public product responses have parseable Product/Offer/BreadcrumbList data and required sampled name/image/price/currency fields. None has brand,GTIN,weight or availability in raw JSON-LD. The current snapshot deliberately selects a small public column set. Rendered Combino adds known1000g/InStock but still no brand. No brand/GTIN is fabricated; the current omission is safer than guessing.
- **Why it matters:** Google receives fewer product facts initially, reducing potential enhancement/identity coverage. Missing recommended fields are not proof of ineligibility. [Google product enhancements](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/product).
- **Recommendation:** Reuse approved canonical brand/identifiers/package facts and a freshness-safe public stock source where available; keep the public-column allowlist and source/runtime agreement. Add shipping/return data only when actual policies support it. Do not invent a barcode or publish private pricing/supplier fields. **Idea:**IDEA-20261026-09.
