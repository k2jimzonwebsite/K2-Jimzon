# Product families and SKU variations

Owner: MAP-023 / IDEA-20261021-03. Accepted approach: link existing SKUs; the owner confirmed flavours as well as weights and explicitly requested continuing implementation on 21 October 2026. This document is the design, not another backlog. All execution status and remaining work live in MAP-023.

## Understanding and constraints

Shoppers find one mother product and choose an existing variation. Each selected combination retains its SKU, price, stock, photos, label facts, Knowledge and order history. The family is a browsing identity; it cannot own batches or become an order line. Staff explicitly group related products; no names, weights or flavours are inferred. Existing brand/category assignments are not changed. A family must share a canonical brand and category, including the same assigned/unassigned state; grouping never repairs missing taxonomy or publication evidence.

Only variation dimensions that actually differ among publicly eligible members appear. A family with Flavour and Weight shows at most those two selectors. A family with another genuine dimension, such as Size or Pack count, uses that staff-defined label; there are no prefilled empty dimensions. A dimension with one distinct value stays in the selected product details, without a redundant selector. Ungrouped and single-visible-SKU products keep their current presentation.

The existing wood/editorial storefront and dense Admin identity remain authoritative. `ui-ux-pro-max`, `impeccable`, `design-taste-frontend` and `emil-design-eng` apply together. No decorative motion or additional libraries. Maintain current catalog scale/loading budgets, one bounded grouping read per refresh rather than per card, existing Admin authorization, mobile/keyboard access and recovery on uncertainty.

## Data and authority

Add separate `product_families` and `product_family_members` relations. A family stores a name, ordered dimension labels and optimistic version. A member references an existing product UUID and an exact object of dimension values. Each product belongs to at most one family; combinations within a family are unique. Bound a family to 100 existing members and six dimensions, with names/labels/values bounded for storage and readable UI. These are input safety limits, not extra rendered selectors.

The canonical signed Admin/SuperAdmin command replaces a family's complete reviewed membership under one transaction. Check current role, signature, exact payload, idempotent receipt, optimistic version, product existence, common taxonomy, duplicate combinations and foreign membership before changing anything. Serialize family changes before product locks, in deterministic order, to avoid simultaneous membership claims. Store an immutable before/after grouping event; never update products, prices, orders, lots, reviews or Website assignments. A zero-member replacement removes the grouping while preserving its event/receipt history. No unauthenticated or direct client writes.

Local qualification found that the captured shared verifier rejects new action domains. The prepared migration therefore admits only `product_family_save` under an exact captured-definition SHA256 guard and verifies unchanged owner/ACL/settings and all other verifier bytes. It preserves the existing 16 KiB signing ceiling; family payloads are bounded to 16,000 bytes. Production authorization must explicitly include this security-boundary addition. Drift refuses the complete transaction. Public grouping uses one bounded, definer read RPC with explicit eligibility/current-taxonomy filtering; underlying family tables have forced RLS and no client grants or policies, rather than an additional public view/table surface.

The public read RPC returns only published Live/Active products with current explicit Website eligibility. Unlisted direct SKU pages retain their existing behavior but do not expose family siblings through an unlisted member. Drafts, unpublished and other-channel products never contribute labels, counts, prices or options. Failed or uninstalled grouping reads fall back to individual SKU listings, without retaining stale grouping. This does not bypass existing catalog/stock failures or make unknown stock buyable.

Catalog/price/stock readiness does not wait for optional grouping. After those existing reads settle, one dynamically loaded validator/reader requests grouping for at most 1,000 eligible website SKUs with a four-second deadline. Validated mapping is added separately; unavailable, oversized or malformed grouping falls back to individual SKUs. Pure small card/search presentation helpers remain separate from the deferred validator and product-page option resolution. The optional featured before/after comparison is loaded only when used, with the existing primary image as its fallback. Both artifact builds retain their existing size limits.

Current common taxonomy is checked on reads as well as saves. If an independent existing Product Master assignment makes family members disagree, public grouping returns no mapping for that family and eligible SKUs remain individual listings. The Admin reader marks the family inconsistent and requires correcting its membership before another grouping save. No new trigger or change to Product Master assignment authority is needed; this transition is part of focused verification.

## Storefront interaction

Group eligible discovery results after SKU relevance/filtering so a search for an exact variant opens that variant and each family appears once. Exact SKU/name matches rank first, direct SKU facts/options next, and family-name-only matches last; the existing catalog sort is preserved within each tier. Ranking chooses the representative; the full independently eligible catalog supplies the family's selectors, option count and price range, even when only one SKU matches the search. Family cards show the family name, an understated options count and Choose options; multi-variant cards do not silently add a representative SKU. Their price uses the eligible member range and never implies a price applies to all options. Card stock describes options, not a fungible stock pool.

On the product page, compact labelled controls sit above quantity and Add to cart. Small sets use wrapping 44px buttons; larger sets use a labelled native select to keep the page short. Only actual dimension values appear. Controls retain visible keyboard focus and selected states beyond colour. No selector header, decorative icon, empty variation row or lengthy instruction block.

Selecting a value chooses a real member, preserving other current choices when a matching combination exists. If preserving the other choices is impossible, the option's accessible and visible label must explicitly include every other differing dimension, for example `500 g · Vanilla`; only choosing that fully labelled real combination changes them. Never silently substitute another flavour or show a fictional Cartesian combination. Existing sold-out options remain inspectable with truthful unavailability and the existing Pasabuy action; other variants' stock never makes the selected SKU buyable. The product URL stays SKU-based, preserving deep links, back/forward and refresh. Variant switches reset quantity/gallery and update SKU-specific title, image, price, ingredients/allergens, Knowledge and availability. No family facts are copied across flavours.

Cart and checkout summaries visibly include the selected dimension values and SKU, even if legacy product names omit them. Different SKUs remain separate lines. Persisted orders continue to use their existing canonical SKU/name snapshots; historical orders never resolve labels from current grouping metadata. No historical order rewrite or new order schema is needed. Existing exact-SKU stock and FEFO code remain the authority.

## Admin interaction and recovery

An administrator selects existing Inventory rows and opens a focused variation editor. It loads any existing family's complete members before editing; a mixed-family selection is refused rather than implicitly moving members. Staff set the mother name, add only needed dimension labels and enter exact values per member. Provide explicit remove-member and ungroup actions. Do not require typed audit prose; the command writes a useful automatic event description.

Pending saves preserve the original actor, literal payload and idempotency key using the established durable-command recovery pattern. Freeze editing during uncertain outcomes; exact retry never creates a new grouping decision. Check actor before dispatch and after response, preserve pending evidence on reload and prevent competing tabs from dispatching simultaneously. A stale family version requires reloading the latest family before a fresh edit; it never overwrites another staff change. Loading, no-selection, missing dependency, permission, validation, conflict and uncertain-save states give concrete next actions.

## Decisions and alternatives

- Accepted: link existing SKU records. Rejected: rewriting the catalog into a new variant engine because it would increase migration risk and duplicate working inventory/order behavior.
- Accepted: staff-defined ordered dimensions, with only differing publicly visible values rendered. Rejected: hardcoded Flavour/Weight/Colour/Size placeholders because they bloat products without those variations.
- Accepted: explicit grouping and per-SKU facts. Rejected: automatic fuzzy-name grouping or inheriting ingredient/allergen/media facts because similar names and packaging do not establish identity.
- Accepted: one grouping boundary, own receipt/event and existing authorization. No production schema apply, data grouping or deployment is authorized by design acceptance alone; the K2 preflight/readiness, exact owner authorization, backup and rollback gates still apply.
- Skeptic review: accepted four objections. Sparse-combination labels must name every changed dimension before selection; search ranking uses a full eligible family independently of matching results; cart/checkout use current labels while historical orders retain canonical existing snapshots/SKU; labels and values are trimmed/NFC normalized, with case-insensitive duplicate labels/values rejected and exact normalized combinations unique. No new business behavior beyond grouping was added.
- Constraint review: accepted the common-taxonomy drift objection. Recheck all members' current taxonomy on reads; inconsistent groups disappear from public grouping, not from the eligible SKU catalog, and Admin must explicitly review membership. Do not extend Product Master with another mutation path.
- User Advocate review: APPROVED, with scroll/focus continuity required. A same-family variation switch updates the SKU URL without page-top scrolling or moving keyboard focus. Quantity/gallery reset while the persistent controls retain their DOM/focus identity. This is covered in rendered verification.
- Integrator/Arbiter: APPROVED. All skeptic, constraint and user-advocate objections are accepted and resolved as recorded above. Proceed with local implementation/verification; this disposition grants no production installation, record grouping or deployment authorization.

## 22 October critique refinements

A family axis with one currently eligible value still identifies the chosen SKU. Render it as a read-only shared-product detail, not a one-option selector. Keep it attached to the selected SKU and do not use it to infer package content or copy facts between members. Admin displays canonical product lifecycle status and the Website channel/publication state as separate values for every member; unknown or ambiguous read state fails closed. Ungrouping requires explicit confirmation naming the member count and stating that it removes only family presentation. Close and Escape ask before discarding a dirty local draft; cancel retains the complete draft. These refinements do not alter SKU inventory, price, product content, publication or order ownership.
