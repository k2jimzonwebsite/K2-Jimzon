## Proposed technical choices and alternatives

The reviewed contract selects a private policy table keyed by the existing UUID `public.categories.id`, plus immutable change events. A missing override means90. This keeps policy writes separate from taxonomy labels and avoids a second product/category register. An ordinary category column would be simpler but couples taxonomy edits to inventory authority and lacks a separate reviewed mutation boundary. A browser/static map cannot provide authoritative concurrent updates or preserve server truth and is rejected. These storage choices are approved design, not installed behavior.

Each policy row holds a nullable integer minimum (NULL means inherited/default; non-NULL must be≥90), monotonic version, actor/time and reason. Clearing an override retains its row and advances the version; physical row deletion is prohibited so clear/recreate cannot reset version and accept an old request. Do not seed any override. Use a foreign key to canonical category identity and a unique category key. Append-only events preserve before/after values and versions. Existing products, quantities, lots, reservations, publication and channel membership are never updated by policy installation or mutation.

## Resolution and eligibility

Resolve the product's `category_id` and traverse `categories.parent_id`. Effective minimum is `max(90, all present category overrides)`. A missing product/category, dangling parent or detected cycle fails closed for eligibility; do not silently substitute90 for malformed taxonomy. Traversal uses visited IDs and a documented bounded hierarchy guard; reaching the guard refuses instead of truncating inheritance. The exact bound is an implementation limit to verify against real taxonomy before activation, not an invented operating fact.

For a fresh mutating command, capture one server clock instant after policy/resource lock acquisition and before eligibility evaluation; derive its Manila date and pass that date internally through all evaluations in that command. Do not accept a browser date. Do not reuse a pre-wait transaction-start date. A separate fresh command re-evaluates, including after midnight; a genuine completed durable retry returns its recorded result before new eligibility checks and creates no duplicate effects. Read-only public projection uses one statement evaluation instant/date. Remaining days are the lot's effective expiry date minus that date. Ordinary eligibility requires days≥effective minimum, plus every existing disposition/location/custody/quantity/reservation/Website criterion. The31–89 band continues to require current genuine clearance evidence under existing expiry/disposition/reversal invalidation rules. If the minimum is120, days90–119 are ineligible; a clearance approval does not bypass that gap. Exactly120 is ordinary eligible. Existing FEFO selects earliest eligible lots rather than earliest physical lots.

Receiving records actual physical stock unchanged. Its available/quarantine decision must use the same effective ordinary minimum;31–89 is quarantine on arrival until the existing separate reviewed clearance workflow qualifies it. Raising a policy does not rewrite old lot disposition or counts: each later public projection and operational eligibility check reads current policy. Fresh stock-consuming confirmation/payment/packing/handover must refuse an allocation that is no longer eligible, preserving the entire commitment and command state. Existing authorized cancellation/release rules remain the only authority to undo commitments; release can decrement reservations even when the lot is ineligible, but may not project released units as sellable until current eligibility passes. No new cancellation or transfer authority follows. Lowering a reviewed override remains≥90 and records a new event/version. A previously quarantined lot remains quarantined after a decrease; only the existing reviewed lot-reconciliation/disposition workflow can restore available status after its current eligibility/permissions/evidence checks. No automatic disposition change follows.

## Concurrency and ownership

Proposed serialization: one transaction-scoped shared policy lock for operations and an exclusive lock for policy/taxonomy mutations. Acquire it at the command boundary before product, listing, balance, lot or reservation locks. The eligibility helper must not acquire a late lock after those locks; callers must prove the boundary order. Shared locks allow normal inventory commands concurrently; policy changes are rare and briefly serialize against them. Product category reassignment and parent edits must participate in the same boundary, because either can change effective policy without editing an override.

Select the strongest required lock mode at entry for the complete command and nested calls. Commands that also mutate taxonomy/policy acquire exclusive mode immediately; prohibit shared-to-exclusive upgrades or late acquisition. Proposed local limits are2seconds for policy-lock waiting and10seconds for the complete command statement, both bounded by the existing host/request deadline if shorter. Timeout refuses with complete rollback and a retryable conflict; do not retry while holding inventory locks. These are proposed verification targets, not measured host SLAs. Test deliberate long transactions and lock-order inversion. Existing global rate/idempotency controls and limits remain intact.

The policy mutation boundary validates actor/AAL2/signature/action/version/reason, acquires exclusive policy lock before taxonomy rows, rejects cycles and invalid values, writes policy/event atomically and preserves all inventory/order/listing rows. Mutating operations support READ COMMITTED only: verify isolation at entry, acquire the policy lock in a separate statement, then resolve policy in a subsequent statement with a fresh snapshot after any wait. Refuse REPEATABLE READ/SERIALIZABLE callers until an independently proven compatible protocol exists. Combining lock acquisition and policy SELECT in one statement is insufficient and prohibited. No direct browser grants or service keys are introduced. Proposed mutation authority reuses the current product-master BFF Admin/SuperAdmin role gate (`server/admin-bff/product-master.js` and `isAdminRole` in `server/admin-bff/supabase.js`), enforced again in the SQL boundary with AAL2 and valid signing. Staff may use existing permitted inventory commands but gains no policy mutation permission. Verify taxonomy mutation callers before activation; a browser-only role gate is insufficient. Activation stays gated until every existing direct/canonical/signed writer and category mutation path is classified and composed. Version conflicts and refusals retain exact before-state. Recovery retains events/data and corrected guards through controlled deactivation/roll-forward rather than restoring vulnerable definitions.

A policy change retains publication flags/Website membership and physical/reserved counts while the next query may project fewer eligible sellable units. “Listing unchanged” refers to those saved listing fields, not a promise of unchanged offer quantity.

Expose distinct content-safe outcomes for retryable policy-lock timeout, malformed taxonomy requiring authorized taxonomy correction, and an ineligible committed allocation requiring the existing reconciliation workflow. Do not map these to a generic no-stock result or expose private policy/actor data publicly. A successful durable retry confirms only the earlier completed action and its historical receipt. The next payment/packing/handover command must run current eligibility anew; that receipt grants no exemption from a raised minimum. These are server/result/runbook requirements, not a new UI design.
