# Milan packing workflow

Owner: MAP-023 / IDEA-20261029-06. Owner-confirmed scope and selected approach, 29 October 2026. **Design review APPROVED; source implemented and locally qualified, migration prepared only. Not installed or deployed.** Unfinished release/real-device work belongs only in MASTER_ACTION_PLAN.md. Evidence and precise limits: `docs/acceptance/MILAN_PACKING_DISCOVERY_20261029.md`.

## Purpose and confirmed requirements

Help the cousin pack physical Italy-to-Philippines boxes without navigating the catalog for every unit. Owner confirms barcode counting, once-per-batch box/lot/expiry details, recoverable packing mistakes, protected dispatched/received history and independent Manila recount. Owner additionally requires optional product-detail completion and per-product +1/−1 controls. The owner explicitly selects **Draft SKU + scanning and +1/−1** over keeping unknown barcodes awaiting later SKU matching.

Assumptions: a few authenticated staff, phone camera or a keyboard-style USB/Bluetooth scanner, online operation, one packaged unit per scan, existing K2 Admin styling and permissions. No paid integration. Testing does not remove or modify current production records.

## Selected workflow

1. Show the current stage clearly: packing boxes in Italy, in transit, or recounting in Manila. Staff choose the shipment and physical box; choose/record actual batch and expiry once for the relevant product lot. Never invent an expiry, price, brand or product claim.
2. A scan identifies a product and adds one packed unit. Expose adjacent labelled +1/−1 controls on the same product/box/lot row. Staff can count manually without scanning every unit. Keep scanned quantities separate from any previously declared expected quantity; an absent target must not require counting first or be represented as a false shipment plan. Retain existing entered expected counts.
3. An unknown barcode becomes one canonical, stock-free, unpublished Draft with a server-assigned K2 SKU and an explicit unfinished label. Show persistent **Details needed** and a route to later product review; missing descriptions, photos or merchandising details do not stop packing. The existing full product-review command requires evidence/photos: do not fabricate its accepted evidence or weaken its review gate to simulate completion. A narrowly scoped packing-Draft entry must retain actual barcode/origin and incomplete status, reusing canonical SKU allocation and duplicate protection.
4. A known barcode reuses its canonical product. Ambiguous identity is an actionable exception, not silent selection of another product. A barcode shared by multiple box/lot lines requires explicit lot context; it cannot silently spill into another box when one fills.
5. Before dispatch, a mistaken count can be reduced, a mistaken line removed from the active packing list, and a shipment cancelled. Retain actor, old/new count, source and correction/cancellation history. Removal does not delete the catalog product or historical records. Counts never go negative. Once dispatched, packing edits are refused; Manila recount/receipt remains its separate controlled workflow.
6. Manila must recount actual units independently. Packing does not change inventory, prices, orders, payments, custody balances, Website listing or publication. Incomplete Drafts remain excluded from public sale until normal review/publication/eligibility gates pass. Physical receiving eligibility and unknown facts retain the existing fail-closed/quarantine rules; no invented facts or bypass grants.

## Command and reliability boundaries

Extend the existing canonical consignment command path rather than reviving `MilanPackingScannerModal.jsx` or creating a second packing/inventory model. Protected commands retain the current session/role/AAL2/CSRF/signature authority. Add only the packing Draft/count/correction/removal/cancellation capabilities needed by this scope; table writes remain inaccessible to the browser.

Each intentional +1/−1 or physical scanner submission receives its own operation identity. Atomic server transactions verify current stage, exact shipment/box/lot/product, permitted integer delta/bounds, duplicate identity and actor, then record the count and audit/receipt together. Concurrent count/cancel/dispatch must serialize on the same shipment boundary, with consistent lock order; failed commands change no partial product/line/count. Creating a Draft and its first packing line/count must not leave a phantom product or unit after partial failure.

Reuse the existing durable actor-scoped command-recovery utilities where suitable: retain the literal body/key on unknown outcomes, stop subsequent dependent work, and retry only the identical command after reauthorization. Never issue a new key merely because a response was lost, reset a saved pending request, or apply a delayed response to another actor/shipment. Unavailable storage or uncertain identity must be explicit before mutation.

Historical replay is distinct from a fresh mutation: after current actor/signature authorization, an exact saved receipt is returned before checking the present shipment stage. A previously committed count remains acknowledged after dispatch/cancellation; a new count at that stage is refused. A receipt confirms that historical decision only. Reload current canonical shipment state before rendering totals, so an old receipt cannot overwrite later counts or resurrect a removed line.

Keyboard-scanner submissions and counter presses must not share camera-frame debounce. Serialize deliberate rapid scans without silently discarding busy reads; show pending work and confirmed counts distinctly and pause visibly if capacity/uncertainty is reached. Camera repeated frames must not count a stationary barcode repeatedly; explain rearming for another identical physical unit. Closing/changing shipment while work is pending must preserve/reconcile it rather than lose or redirect it. No network or offline acceptance claim is made by an animation or local optimistic number.

The actor-scoped pending journal covers **all accepted operations, including unsent queued scans**, not just its in-flight head. A submission is accepted only after its original actor, shipment, box/lot/product-or-code, exact delta/body and unique key are persisted and read back. Cap the journal at **32 pending operations**. On full/unavailable storage, visibly pause input, retain the unaccepted field/code, and confirm neither acceptance nor count. Cross-tab serialization and exact journal comparison must preserve every accepted operation when another tab updates it. Process in order and remove only the validated acknowledged head; unknown outcomes stop progress and keep the head/tail unchanged. Scope changes/closing cannot discard this journal. On reload, offer explicit recovery with the original actor/context and keys; do not silently create replacement operations or reassign queued scans to the currently selected box. Include a burst→interrupt/reload-before-send→resume test proving every accepted operation is applied exactly once with its original context. This is recovery for the same canonical consignment command stream, not an offline inventory ledger.

Packing closure preserves actual counted quantities and the independent expected-plan facts. If an existing plan differs, require an explicit, intelligible acknowledgement rather than falsifying expected quantities or silently changing a custody record. Cancellation/removal remains reversible/history-preserving where normal product recovery permits; no hard purge is part of this feature.

Closure/removal/cancellation review carries an expected shipment packing revision or an equivalent exact ordered snapshot of the reviewed line identities/counts. Under the same shipment lock, the server refuses a stale review without mutation and asks staff to review current counts again. Every admitted old/new line/count writer must participate in that revision/snapshot boundary; a UI-only token is insufficient. Count-versus-close/cancel races must prove either the reviewed mutation commits first or the stale decision refuses, never acknowledgement of a different discrepancy.

Unfinished product status uses canonical `products.is_human_reviewed=false`, `published=false` and Draft lifecycle, not a second review authority. **Details needed** cannot be cleared by a name/description-only edit. The later completion action is the existing reasoned/versioned Product Master human-review decision by its authorized Admin/SuperAdmin, retaining its canonical required-field/category controls and explicit `is_human_reviewed=true` intent. Link that existing editor from packing; do not mark review automatically on opening it, attaching an image, naming the Draft or receiving goods. Existing database publication eligibility must still refuse an unreviewed product. Staff remain responsible for factual identification; the temporary identity must be visibly called out during review, with no fake intake-photo/evidence approvals. Verify an ordinary field save leaves the Draft unreviewed and unpublished, and only an explicit authorized review plus existing publication/Website gates can make it public. This does not relax the stricter separate normal intake Draft-creation evidence gate.

## UI and accessibility

Preserve Admin product tokens, Source Sans controls, readable density, mobile usability and >=44px touch targets. Use an inline packing workspace for frequent work; scanning and manual counters must be equally available. Show product name when known, stable SKU/barcode, box/lot, packed count and unfinished-detail status. Do not show a misleading red Manila shortage while still packing in Italy. Keep plain action labels and actionable duplicate/unknown/overage/retry errors. Respect reduced motion; no repeated animations or focus loss on each scan. Verify mobile/desktop/keyboard/large text and actual light/dark behavior in one initial visual round plus at most one confirmation round.
